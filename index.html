Race weekend begins in

September 4–5, 2027

Hydroplane racing comes to the Miramichi River. HRL boats run at Richey Wharf for two days over the Labour Day weekend of 2027 — right downtown, with the Centennial Bridge and the river as the backdrop.

We're putting the details together now — the race schedule, tickets and everything happening on shore. Check back here, and follow along for news as the event takes shape.
